01 — About

A bit about how I work

I started with the Treehouse Front End Web Development Techdegree — nine modules that took me from raw HTML/CSS through JavaScript and into full front-end applications. Since finishing, I've been building independent practice projects that go further: full-stack dashboards, API-driven apps, and small experiments — all self-directed and built purely for learning, not for profit.

I like practice projects modeled on real-world use cases — whether that's a mock ticketing system patterned after internal support tools, a dashboard that tracks live SpaceX launches, or a one-question weather app that just tells you if it's raining. Currently focused on React, TypeScript, and Node/Express.
